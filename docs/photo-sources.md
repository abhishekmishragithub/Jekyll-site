# About photo selection

The owner supplied and explicitly approved the `for-website` folder for publication. Every photo below was read from that folder. None was sourced from the separate reference-only folder. Originals and unused photos remain local.

This is a curated selection rather than a dump of repeated shots. Captions describe visible content; event names/dates are used only where supported by the photograph or its supplied filename. No identities are inferred for other people.

Web copies are auto-oriented, proportionally resized and encoded as WebP, with EXIF and other metadata removed. Small copies have a 640px maximum edge; enlarged copies have a 1600px maximum edge. Photos are not retouched or generatively altered. The collage follows the monochrome site treatment; the enlarged viewer shows the photos in their original colour.
